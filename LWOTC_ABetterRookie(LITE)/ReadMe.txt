[h1]A Better Rookie for Long War of The Chosen (LITE)[/h1]
Lightly Buffed soldier and other player types' abilities, characteristics, growth, and more.

Commander we know you have been away and before you start thinking that we spent all of our time scavenging wind turbines to build our flying cave base we wanted you to know that we have been dabbling a bit with bioengineering.  

Our rookies come from the solid stock of our orginal XCOM gene-modified and psi-trained soldiers and then trained up and made ready for the day you returned to once again wage war against alien forces. 

This mod, created in the same vein as "A Better Start" starts your rookies out with more mobility, HP, offense (aim), defense, and WILL [strike](they have the WILL of Gods... no more will PVT. G. PILE piss his pants because he got shot at after stepping off the Skyranger).[/strike] (WILL is bumped up from 40 to 70 which still allows for the chance for PVT PILE to wet himself at the site of blood.)

[i]*This mod works at all gamelevel difficulties.[/i]

[h1]Under The Hood[/h1]
The changes made span 4 different ini files including the soldier skills, character stats, LW soldier skills, and xp data. 
[list]
[*][b]Character Stats -[/b] All of your men, women, and sparks have greater health, mobility, offense, defense, WILL, crit, etc. - LITE: mobility, hp, defense, etc. have been considerably lessened from full version.
[*][b]Soldier Skills -[/b] cool-downs and charges have primarily been tweaked so that you can use abilities more often during an engagement. LITE: all cool downs have only been reduced by 1 turn without any 0 cool downs. ability charges have only been increased by 1 in most cases. Ability damage modifiers have also been reduced.
[*][b]XP/Rank -[/b] the criteria for promotion has been reduced so that you can climb the ranks faster and unlock those awesome perks. - LITE: rank progression is not as fast as full version but better than vanilla.
[/list]

[h1]DIY Modding[/h1]
If there are aspects of this mod that you hate or things you wished were just a bit more buffed, then open the file(s) below in a text editor and scan through settings. My mods are nothing more than configuration file overrides with no coding. A word of caution about editing the downloaded files in your workshop directory. If I ever update this mod your changes will get replaced with a fresh copy of my files. I recommend copying the modified file to another folder that you can swap back in after an update.
[code]C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\workshop\content\268500\2053919782\Config\
XComGameData_CharacterStats.ini
XComGameData _SoldierSkills.ini
XComGameData _XpData.ini
XComLW_Overhaul.ini[/code]
[i]*This is assuming your running windows and steamapps are on your c:\ drive.[/i]
